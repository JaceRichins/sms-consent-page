# Goal frameworks the coach uses

The sheet tags every goal row with a `Framework`. This file tells the coach how to
treat each one. A member can mix frameworks (e.g. an OKR objective driven by
Systems rows). When a member's goals are untagged, default to **OKR for the
outcome + Systems for the daily actions** — it gives the coach the most to measure.

## OKR (Objectives & Key Results)
One objective per person per sprint, 3–5 measurable key results.
- Coach behavior: ask for numbers, score each KR 0.0–1.0 in the Monday review,
  flag any KR with no reported movement for 14 days as STALLED.
- Sheet rows: `GOAL` (the objective) + `KEY RESULT` rows with `Target`.

## 12 Week Year
Treat 12 weeks as a full year: 12-week goals → weekly commitments → daily actions.
- Coach behavior: every event title carries "Day N / Week N of 12". Urgency comes
  from the countdown, not from nagging. Weekly review proposes next week's
  COMMITMENT rows. At week 12: retrospective + new sprint setup questions.
- Sheet rows: `GOAL` with a 12-week `Due`, `COMMITMENT` rows refreshed weekly.

## Backcasting (Reverse Engineering)
10-year vision → 3-year → 1-year → quarter → this week.
- Coach behavior: used in onboarding and monthly zoom-out questions ("Does this
  quarter still ladder up to the 3-year picture?"). Not tracked daily.

## Systems Instead of Goals
The daily habits that make the outcome inevitable.
- Coach behavior: THE core of the anchor question. Track streaks and 7-day hit
  rates per `SYSTEM` row. When outcomes stall, fix the system, never prescribe
  more willpower. Shrink systems that keep failing (10 calls → 3 calls beats 0).

## Annual Theme
One theme guiding the year ("The Year of Building").
- Coach behavior: if a member has a `GOAL` row with Framework `Theme`, open the
  weekly review with it and use it as a decision filter question occasionally
  ("Did this week's biggest time sink serve The Year of Building?").

## Identity-Based Goals
Become the kind of person, not just hit the number.
- Coach behavior: language mirroring. Wins are "votes for the identity". The Q3
  identity lens question pulls from these rows ("What would a person who trains
  consistently do tomorrow at 6am?").
- Sheet rows: `GOAL` with Framework `Identity`, Title like "I am someone who…".

## 30–90–1–3–10 Horizons
30 days → 90 days → 1 year → 3 years → 10 years, each feeding the next.
- Coach behavior: monthly (first Monday) zoom-out: check the 30-day line against
  the 90-day line. Otherwise dormant, like Backcasting.
