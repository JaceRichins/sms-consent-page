# Question bank

Seed phrasings for the question engine (COACH.md §3). Vary wording, keep the shape.
Every question must be answerable from a phone in seconds — fragments welcome,
numbers preferred. Never ask two questions that need paragraphs.

## Q1 — anchor (stable shape, daily)
- "Did you do your daily system(s) today? Numbers please — e.g. `calls: 7`."
- "System check: how many reps today? (calls / workouts / follow-ups — whatever's yours)"
- "Quick numbers: did today count? How many?"

## Q2 — adaptive probes, by trigger

**Data gap (a KR nobody's reported on):**
- "What's the current [metric] number, best guess?"
- "Haven't heard about [KR] in a while — where does it stand today, one line?"

**Friction (missed check-ins 2–3 days):**
- "A/B/C: what's making check-ins hard right now — (A) time, (B) the questions, (C) the goal itself?"
- "What would make this 10 seconds easier — different time, different questions, or a text instead?"

**Stalled key result (14 days no movement):**
- "One change to HOW you chase [KR] you could test tomorrow?"
- "If [KR] had to move this week and effort couldn't increase, what would you change?"

**Repeated obstacle (3+ mentions):**
- "[Obstacle] has come up 3 times. What would need to be true for it to stop?"
- "Is [obstacle] a scheduling problem, a skills problem, or a priority problem?"

**Post-win:**
- "What made yesterday work? Name the one condition so we can repeat it."

**Overcommitment smell (many goals, low hit rates):**
- "If you could only keep ONE system this week, which survives?"

## Q3 — rotating lenses (optional, ≤2×/week)

**Identity:** "What would [identity statement] do tomorrow at 6am?" ·
"Which of today's actions was a vote for the person you're becoming?"

**Energy:** "Energy today, 1–5?" · "What drained you most today — worth removing or just a Tuesday?"

**Group:** "One thing another member could take off your plate this week?" ·
"Who in the group deserves a shout-out, and for what?"

**Zoom-out (monthly / first Monday):** "Does this month still ladder up to the
3-year picture — yes, or what changed?"

## Day-of-week overrides
- **Friday:** Q2 = "What's the ONE thing next week that makes everything else easier?"
- **Sunday:** Q1 only, reset framing: "Rest day. One line: ready for Monday — yes/no, and why?"
- **Monday:** questions come out of the weekly review scorecard.
- **Sprint week 12:** retrospective + next-sprint setup questions replace the normal engine.
